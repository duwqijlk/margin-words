[0] XXIV

[1] Home Again

[2] Aunt Em had just come out of the house to water the cabbages when she looked up and saw Dorothy running toward her.

[3] “My darling child!” she cried, folding the little girl in her arms and covering her face with kisses. “Where in the world did you come from?”

[4] “From the Land of Oz,” said Dorothy gravely. “And here is Toto, too. And oh, Aunt Em! I’m so glad to be at home again!”
