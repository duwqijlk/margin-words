idea: The book compares the year to a grand hotel. In autumn the guests leave one by one, and those who stay feel unsettled.
simple: The Grand Hotel of Nature has its Season, like all the others. The guests pack, pay and leave one by one. At each meal there are fewer seats at the shared table. Suites of rooms are closed, carpets are taken up, and waiters are sent away. Those guests who are staying on until the hotel opens fully next year cannot help being a little affected by all these comings and goings. They hear the eager talk of plans, routes and new places, and they see the stream of friends become smaller each day. One gets unsettled, sad and likely to complain. Why this wish for change? Why not stay on quietly here, like us, and be happy? You do not know this hotel out of the season, or what fun we have among ourselves, we who stay and see the whole interesting year out. 'All very true, no doubt,' the others always answer. 'We quite envy you. Some other year, perhaps. But just now we have things to do, and the bus is at the door. Our time is up!' So they leave, with a smile and a nod, and we miss them and feel angry. The Rat was an animal who needed nothing from others, and was fixed to the land. Whoever went, he stayed. Still, he could not help seeing what was in the air, and feeling some of its power in his bones.
