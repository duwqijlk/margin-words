ctx: When Toad found himself immured in a dank and noisome dungeon
idea: Toad is in the dungeon and is very sad. He thinks it is the end of everything, and he is sorry for himself.
simple: Toad found himself shut in a cold, wet dungeon with a bad smell. He knew that all the dark of an old castle lay between him and the outside world. In that world there was sunshine, and there were good high roads, where he had lately been so happy, playing as if he had bought every road in England. He threw himself down on the floor at full length, and cried bitter tears, and gave himself up to dark despair. 'This is the end of everything,' he said, 'or at least it is the end of the career of Toad, which is the same thing. The popular and handsome Toad, the rich and kind-to-guests Toad, the Toad so free and careless and gay! How can I hope ever to be free again,' he said, 'when I have been put in prison so fairly? I stole such a handsome motorcar in such a bold way, and I gave such wild and clever cheek to so many fat, red-faced policemen!' (Here his crying stopped his words.) 'What a stupid animal I was,' he said. 'Now I must sit in this dungeon until the people who were proud to say they knew me have forgotten the very name of Toad! O wise old Badger!' he said, 'O clever, intelligent Rat and sensible Mole! What sound judgments, what knowledge of men and things you have! O unhappy and left-alone Toad!' He spent his days and nights like this for several weeks. He would not eat his meals or the light snacks between them. But the old gaoler knew that Toad's pockets were full, and he often said that many comforts, and even luxuries, could be sent in from outside, for a price.
hard: immured; dank; noisome; medieval; disporting; at large; audacious; lurid; languish; forsaken; lamentations; gaoler; well lined
