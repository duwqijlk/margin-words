make out|To see or understand something with some effort. Here, to find out what a thing is.
leave off|To stop doing something.
wind up|To wind thread round and round into a ball.
catch up|To pick up quickly. Here, Alice takes the kitten up in her hands.
make up one's mind|To decide.
set out|To start a trip or a walk.
take one's breath away|To surprise someone so much that they cannot speak for a moment.
get on|To do something and make progress. Here, "didn't get on very fast" means she did not make much progress.
at any rate|Anyway; whatever else is true.
come undone|To open or come loose; to stop being tied or wound.
get in the way|To be in the place where someone wants to go, so that they cannot pass easily.
keep up with|To go as fast as someone else, so as not to fall behind.
in vain|Without success; with no result.
all the better|Even better. It adds more to the idea that something is good.
take care|To be careful.
give up|To stop trying, because it is too hard.
have any hand in|To take part in something.
to and fro|Moving one way and then back again.
in time|After some time; when enough time has gone by.
no use|Not helpful; there is no good in doing it.
on purpose|Not by chance; meaning to do it.
come off|To come away from the place where it was fixed.
beg one's pardon|A polite way to say "Please say that again" or "I am sorry".
for fear of|Because you are afraid that something bad will happen.
I declare|A way to say strongly that something is true, often when you are surprised.
out of breath|Breathing very fast after running, so that it is hard to speak.
turn out|To point outwards, away from each other.
hold one's tongue|To keep quiet; to say nothing.
all the same|Still; in spite of that.
by the way|A way to add a new thing that you remember while talking.
take hold of|To take something in your hand and keep it there.
set off|To start a trip or to begin to go.
take turns|To do something one after the other, in order.
find fault with|To say that someone or something is bad or has done wrong.
catch a crab|In rowing, to get the oar stuck in the water by mistake. Here the Sheep says Alice will do this.
make believe|To pretend; to act as if something is true.
look on|To watch without taking part.
break off|To stop speaking suddenly.
