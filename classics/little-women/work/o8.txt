blowzy|Red in the face, with hair that is not in good order.
dishevelled|Not in good order. It is used for hair or clothes.
frizzled|Made into many tiny rings, as hair is.
turnover|A small hot food with a soft cover and something sweet inside.
infirm|Weak or not well. Here it is used for old, hurt things that a girl plays with and keeps.
contraband|Not allowed by the rules or the law. Here the teacher uses it for a green fruit that is not allowed at school.
ferrule|To hit a child's hand with a flat piece of wood, as a school way to make the child sorry.
rigmarole|A long, silly story that goes on and on. Here it is the name of a game in which each person adds to a story.
hemlock|A kind of tree with soft green leaves that stay green all year, and with small, hard cases that hold its seed.
ninepence|A small amount of money; nine small pieces of old English money.
mandarin|A play man whose head moves up and down.
pendulum|The part that moves from side to side in a machine that tells the time, and keeps it going.
camphor|A white thing with a sharp, strong feel in the nose, used in medicine.
jungfrau|A word from another language for a young girl. Here it is the name for the cold white girl figure that Jo and Laurie made in the garden.
whatnot|A light stand with open places, one over the other, for small things.
hansom|A light coach, pulled by one horse, that anyone can pay to ride in. It moves on two big round parts, and the driver sits up behind.
barouche|A fine open coach, pulled by horses, with a top that can be pushed back.
dowagers|Old ladies of high position, often women whose husbands have died.
meerschaum|A soft, white stone. Here it means a thing for smoking made from this stone.
hyacinth|A spring flower, sweet to the nose, that grows from a round thing under the ground.
inflammable|Easy to set on fire. Here it means very easy to move to strong feeling.
perjured|Having broken a promise. Here it is a joke about a small child who has broken his word.
grotto|A small, room-like place in a hill or rock; also a cool place in a garden made to look like one.
vista|A long view. Here it is a long look back over many years.
