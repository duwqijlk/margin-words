drab|A brown colour that is not bright.
redoubtable|Strong, and a little hard to face; a person you must respect.
bridled|Lifted the head high and showed anger because of something said.
sacque|A coat that a woman wore, not close to the body.
frizzled|Made into small, round pieces of hair.
mignonette|A garden plant with small, sweet flower.
larkspur|A tall garden plant with blue or white flower.
earwiggy|Full of earwigs, which are small insects with sharp parts at the back.
troubadour|A man who made love songs and played music long ago.
nosegay|A small group of sweet flower.
aromatic|Sweet to the nose, in a strong way.
mandarin|A toy man whose head moves up and down.
pewmonia|A servant's way of saying a very bad sickness of the chest.
pendulum|The part of a big time machine that goes from side to side and keeps it going.
camphor|A white thing with a strong nose-hurting power, used as medicine.
rosary|A string of round pieces that some people use when they speak to God.
stampede|A sudden run of many people at once, with a lot of noise.
jungfrau|A word from another language for a young girl. Here it is the name given to a girl made of ice.
perversity|A wish to do what is not wanted of you.
abject|Very low, with no good opinion of oneself.
moire|A silk cloth that looks like water moving.
salver|A flat metal thing for carrying glasses or food.
afflatus|A strong flow of ideas that seems to come from a higher power.
pungent|Having a strong, sharp taste or nose feeling.
mantua|A woman's dress that is not close to the body. A 'mantua-maker' makes dresses.
hansom|A light car with two big round parts under it, pulled by one horse.
barouche|A fine open car with four big round parts under it, pulled by horses.
woodbine|A plant that goes up walls, with sweet flower.
meerschaum|A soft, white stone used to make a thing for smoking.
hyacinth|A spring flower that is sweet to the nose.
standish|A stand that holds the things used for writing on a table.
bashful|Not comfortable with people; easily upset in front of them.
lapping|The soft sound of small water hitting the edge of the sea.
piquante|Lively and a little strong, in a nice way.
lustre|A bright light that makes a thing look more important.
panniers|Large bags on each side of a horse to carry things.
charred|Burned black on the outside.
benediction|A prayer asking God to be kind to people.
wooing|Trying to win the love of a person whom one hopes to be married to.
bootjack|A tool that holds a shoe so that one can pull one's foot out.
hustled|Pushed hard by people who are in a great rush.
voluminous|Very long, with many pages.
hobbledehoy|A boy who is not yet a man and who moves in a way that is not nice to see.
sheaf|A group of plants cut and held together.
teutonic|From the land of the Germans.
